gltf-progressive stress demo LOD ceiling controller (commit 44b0d1a): the ceiling parked ~60fps regardless of targetFps because (1) minCeil was 2 but in the 3-LOD system (_use3LodSystem true) the ceiling INDEX maps to actual LOD via [0,2,4], so index 2 == LOD4 == MAX detail => floor of 2 = NO effective cap, controller couldn't reclaim frames. Fixed to minCeil=1 (=LOD2 cap, still leaves instanced-LOD0-vs-LOD2 near/far distinction). (2) anti-windup latch _ceilFloorFromFps decayed only at target+25 and blocked all raises, freezing low. Fixed: decay at target+10 over 60 frames, and a bigSurplus (>target+10) bypasses the raise-block so a LOW target genuinely yields MORE detail. Witnessed: target=30/100ent -> ceiling 5 (full) fps 99 floor null; target=140/500ent -> ceiling 1 (max instancing) floor 2. KEY INSIGHT: higher ceiling index = MORE detail; in 3-LOD mode the index->actual-LOD remap [0,2,4] means a 'minimum ceiling' of 2 is actually maximum quality, a trap. The user's 'every block its own flat color' was a DOWNSTREAM symptom of the ceiling stuck low (forcing all models to the instanced vertex-color-only FAR tier, MeshLambertMaterial vertexColors no map); once the ceiling tracks target and climbs to textured LODs (0:0:2/0:0:4 have UV+texture no vertex-color; 0:0:0 unskinned FAR has varied vertex color but .atlas variants collapse to 1 distinct color), textured models render and flat blocks disappear. There IS a parallel .atlas asset set (output_*.atlas/) alongside each output_*/ dir.
